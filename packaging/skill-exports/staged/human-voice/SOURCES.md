# Sources

The Dignity Standard is built on published guidance and research from the organizations below. Each rule and principle file lists the sources it draws on in its `sources:` frontmatter, using the IDs on this page. When a rule changes because of a source, the change is recorded in `okf/log.md`.

We credit these organizations for the work behind the standard. Their inclusion here means we drew on their published material; it does not mean they endorse this project.

## Cited

### samhsa-2014
Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration. *SAMHSA's Concept of Trauma and Guidance for a Trauma-Informed Approach.* HHS Publication No. (SMA) 14-4884, 2014.
Used for: the Six Principles and the Four R's (`okf/trauma-informed/six-principles.md`, `okf/trauma-informed/four-rs.md`).

### plain-language-guidelines
Plain Language Action and Information Network (PLAIN). *Federal Plain Language Guidelines.* March 2011, Revision 1, May 2011. https://www.plainlanguage.gov/guidelines/
Used for: stating the purpose and bottom line first, with background toward the end (`okf/standard/rules/cognitive-accessibility.md`). The Plain Writing Act of 2010 binds U.S. federal agencies; we cite the guidelines as a published standard to align with, not as a legal requirement for other organizations.

### national-council-ti-checklist
National Council for Mental Wellbeing. *Agency-Wide Communication Checklist* (Fostering Resilience and Recovery: Trauma-Informed Primary Care), adapted from material developed by Trauma Informed Oregon. https://www.thenationalcouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Agency-Wide-Communication-Checklist.pdf
Used for: clear, direct, transparent messages, and clear timelines and instructions when a message asks the reader to act (`okf/standard/rules/cognitive-accessibility.md`).

### de-berker-2016
de Berker, A. O., Rutledge, R. B., Mathys, C., Marshall, L., Cross, G. F., Dolan, R. J., & Bestmann, S. (2016). Computations of uncertainty mediate acute stress responses in humans. *Nature Communications*, 7, 10996.
Used for: the reasoning behind putting bad news first. Subjects facing a 50% chance of a shock showed more stress than those certain of a shock or certain of none. Applying this laboratory finding to message structure is our inference, not the authors' claim (`okf/trauma-informed/six-principles.md`).

### goldendoodle-v1
GoldenDoodle AI production system prompt, V1 (2025 to 2026). Source of the "No Surprises" rule (state the purpose gently but immediately), the Reasoning Bridge, and clear next steps, carried into this standard.

### dart-bond
Dart Center for Journalism and Trauma, and Bond (UK) guidance on ethical storytelling and consent, as summarized in `okf/trauma-informed/ti-guide-additions-dart-bond-samhsa.md`.
